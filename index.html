The Story of Bagiffy

Founded at the intersection of modern luxury design and meticulous traditional engineering, Bagiffy specializes in empowering modern women with luxury statements that embody absolute poise.

Based at our flagship design studio located at 93 A/S Farooq-e-Azam Street, Samanabad, Lahore, every single piece is cut from curated exquisite fabrics and premium leathers, treated with high-grade metal alloy accessories, and checked against rigid standards before deployment.

Whether navigating high-profile boardroom corporate ventures, celebrating festive wedding ceremonies, or requiring sleek functionality through an everyday Tote, Bagiffy caters flawlessly to every aesthetic appetite across Pakistan.
